One-time setup

  1. Download the DoorLoop Tours extension and unzip it.
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.
  4. Log in to DoorLoop in the same browser, then click a tour above.

On any DoorLoop page that has tours, a Tours button appears in the bottom-right corner. New and edited tours show up automatically; you only reinstall when the extension itself changes.

Drafts

Tours that aren't live yet. Only people who turn on Show drafts see them on DoorLoop, in that browser: turn on · turn off. Trying a draft below turns it on too.

To make a draft live, open it with Approve on GitHub, change "status": "draft" to "status": "live", and commit. It goes live for everyone once published.
