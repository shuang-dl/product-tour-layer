Tour builder: concept

A clickable draft of how tours could be built visually instead of by writing JSON. Nothing here is saved or published.

Two ways to build. On the demo app, right here: nothing to install. Or on live DoorLoop: the builder opens on top of your own DoorLoop tab through the DoorLoop Tours extension (version 0.3.0 or later), since DoorLoop can't be shown inside another site.
